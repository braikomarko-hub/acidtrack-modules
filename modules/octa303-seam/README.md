# OCTA303 SEAM — the hybrid OCTA-303

The ColdFire keeps the knobs, the sequencer, the envelopes, the accent, the slide and the VCO; the DSP renders the
rest of the voice (the resonance loop with its high-pass, the ladder clip, the four-stage ladder, the VCA input
pair, the level, the OTA, the VCA, pin 6, the output coupling) at the per-track source seam, before the stock AMP,
FX1 and FX2. A voice with FM, MUFF, a DIST carrier or DRIV ≠ 0 plays on the ColdFire as before.

Author: MaMarko (Marko Brajković, [braikomarko-hub](https://github.com/braikomarko-hub)): the idea, the listening and the tests on the unit. **The code was written by Claude (Anthropic).**

- **The seam** is ANALOG BD's: the stock `move a,x:>$20e` (payload A `P:0x39c`, B `P:0x1a2`) is a jsr into
  `o3h.asm`. A record with the signature renders into `X:0` and continues after the source (A `P:0x426`, B
  `P:0x221`); any other record returns into the stock source untouched.
- **The record** (octa303.s, `O3_SEAM`): format in `tools/octa303/ibrido3/hyb.py`.
- **`o3h.asm`** is generated by `tools/octa303/ibrido3/gen_o3h.py`: integer-exact against `ref303`, only
  instruction forms with a stock site or a CHIP.md register row. Pin 6's tables are the section's ptable (the stock
  curve bank when DJ EQ is out of the remix).
- **Memory**: X `$7c00..$7c4b` (four voices' 16 state words, the piece's control block), in stock's unreferenced
  `X:$7a92-$7fff`.

## Known limits (review, 19 Nov 2026)

- **`make cycles` does not count it** (hook sections are not in the figure). One voice costs about as much as
  DISTORX + FET76 together on that core: put one hybrid 303 in T1–T4 and one in T5–T8, not two on the same half,
  until it is measured on the unit.
- **A note that switches between the DSP and the ColdFire** (a DIST, DRIV, FM or MUFF note next to a plain one)
  loses the samples of the frame it switches in: the old note's tail is cut there (a click is possible).
- **The P tables of DISTORX, dB666 and FET76** move to the stock curve bank (X:$4840..) in a remix without DJ EQ,
  and are read with `x:(` there: the same data, a place stock's DJ EQ reads every sample; not yet run on a unit.
