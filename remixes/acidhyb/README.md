# aCiDhYb

aCiDtRacK (V96) with the hybrid OCTA303: `OCTA303 SEAM` puts the voice's resonance loop, ladder, VCA pair, level,
OTA, VCA, pin 6 and output coupling on the DSP at the track's source seam; the ColdFire keeps the knobs, the
sequencer, the envelopes, the accent, the slide and the VCO. A voice with FM, MUFF, a DIST carrier or DRIV plays
entirely on the ColdFire, as in aCiDtRacK. COMPRESSOR, LO-FI and DJ EQ are left out to make room for the DSP code.

## Where it has run

Not yet on a unit (19 Nov 2026): the DSP half on the dsp56300 emulator, the ColdFire half in cfsim
(`tools/octa303/ibrido3/`).
